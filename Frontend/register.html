How to share files from Google Drive
  1. Go to drive.google.com and upload your file (photo as JPG/PNG, resume as PDF)
  2. Right-click the file → click "Get link"
  3. Under General access, change "Restricted" to "Anyone with the link"
  4. Set permission to "Viewer"
  5. Click "Copy link" and paste it below
Links look like: https://drive.google.com/file/d/XXXX/view?usp=sharing
